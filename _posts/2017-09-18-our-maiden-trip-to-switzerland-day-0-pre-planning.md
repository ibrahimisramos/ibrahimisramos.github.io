<p>Our German Course was to start the next week and with accommodation settled, we decided that we should do some traveling prior hand before our project started.</p>
<p>One was reluctant to go France, citing Safety issues. In the sudden twist of events, we eventually agreed to go Switzerland. One of them, Nicholas wanted to go to Lucerne, a popular spot for tourists, one of them wanted to go Switzerland for chocolates and the other had no preferences. For me, I wanted to go see the Alps and one of my secondary school classmate, Kelda, suggested me to go Jungfrau as she said it was very nice. A search of the price showed that the ticket to Jungfrau alone is 206.40 Frank, which is 287 SGD.</p>
<p>So we planned our itinerary to be visiting Lucerne for a day, 1 day to Interlaken Ost and the last day going up to Jungfrau and the very same night returning back to Karlsruhe.</p>
<p>So, we intended to take a train there and booked it through DB office at Karlsruhe Hbf( main train station). We also got a 3 month special Bahn Card 25for 19 euros, as they were celebrating their 25th Anniversary of the Bahn card and with that, we enjoy 25% off train tickets where any part of Germany is involved as the Arrival or Destination. It was quite last minute and the prices were pretty high and we paid 120 euro for the train ticket + Bahn Card.</p>
<p>For Accommodation, we intended to stay at Jugendherberge Youth Hostel at both places as it was cheaper and we would get a discount since we have the Youth Hostel Card.</p>
<p>The night before, we slept late around 2am packing for our trip and researching exactly what to do there.</p>
<p>We had to wake up at 5am to leave by 6-6.30am for our train at 7.50am as it was our first trip and we didn’t want to be late. Let the journey begin</p>
