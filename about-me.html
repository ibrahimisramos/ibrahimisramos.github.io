Personality

My CliftonStrengths are Connectedness, Input, Communication, Strategic, and Individualisation — which, translated out of corporate-speak, means I'm the person who collects a lot of information, sees how it connects to the bigger picture, and then can't stop talking about it. It's a useful combination for security work (lots of disparate signals, one underlying story) and a slightly less useful combination at parties.

I'm happily married to Divyasri, who keeps me grounded and occasionally has to remind me that not every conversation needs to become a structured framework.

I read self-help and personal development books the way some people watch true crime — a little too enthusiastically, and with strong opinions about which authors are overrated.

Training for the Penang Bridge Marathon
December

I am training for my first full marathon (42km) across the Penang Bridge. The slightly absurd part: I don't actually enjoy running, and I'm coming back from an ankle injury from a half marathon I ran the year before. My training plan is deliberately running-light — lots of strength work, cycling, and swimming first — before I let myself near a track. A run/walk strategy on race day is the plan, and finishing is the goal, not a personal best.
