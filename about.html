Hello! Ibrahim here. I'm a Penetration Tester and Cyber Security Consultant at Vantage Point Security, where I test web, API, mobile and thick-client applications. I hold OSCP+, BSCP, CRT, BTL1, CIPT and Google ACE. I graduated from the National University of Singapore with a Bachelor's in Information Security, after a Diploma in Cyber Security and Forensics at Nanyang Polytechnic.

Before going offensive, I spent my early career on the blue side in the Ministry of Home Affairs and Cyber Security Agency SOCs. Seeing attacks from the defender's chair still shapes how I write findings.

Beyond the technical side, I love to learn new things and impact youth through The Astronauts Collective, a charity I volunteer with. In my free time I read self-help books, head to BFT, watch dramas and plan my next trip.

Personality

My CliftonStrengths are Connectedness, Input, Communication, Strategic and Individualisation. Translated out of corporate-speak, I collect a lot of information, see how it connects to the bigger picture, and then can't stop talking about it. Useful for security work (lots of disparate signals, one underlying story), slightly less useful at parties.

I'm happily married to Divyasri, who keeps me grounded and occasionally reminds me that not every conversation needs to become a structured framework.

I read self-help and personal development books the way some people watch true crime: a little too enthusiastically, and with strong opinions about which authors are overrated.
