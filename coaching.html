I've been where you are

I didn't start out with a plan. Each step below began with a belief that held me back, and a choice to rewire it.

  1. 2015

    Nanyang Polytechnic

    I got 8 distinctions and could have gone to JC. I chose poly against my family's wishes, won a scholarship, graduated with a 3.97 GPA and went on to university on another scholarship.

    "Good grades mean JC. Poly is the lesser path."

    The right path is the one that fits you, not the one everyone expects.

  2. 2017

    CSA SOC intern

    Nobody wanted to emcee the Chinese New Year celebration, so on my last day I did it, in Mandarin, without being fluent and without ever having emceed before. I had so much fun.

    "I've never done this before, so I'm not the right person."

    Say yes first. You become the right person by doing it.

  3. 2019

    MHA SOC analyst

    I networked at an event, applied with my resume, and became the first PES fit (B1) person to work in that cyber security operations centre, in the field I'd studied for.

    "People like me don't get into places like that."

    Apply anyway. Let them be the ones to say no.

  4. 2020

    NUS, Information Security

    I joined the University Scholars Programme to stretch myself, even though I thought I was bad at English and writing. Surrounded by top students from RI, Hwa Chong and VJC, I felt like an imposter. I had a panic attack over an essay and rushed to the NUS counsellor, even though I'd been told seeing a counsellor would be a black stain on my career, and a parent had advised me to deal with things myself. One semester my GPA fell below what my scholarship needed. After my first Covid semester, struggling with maths, my strongest subject, I almost quit.

    "Everyone here is smarter than me. I don't belong."

    I made friends with people better than me, and learned from them instead of comparing myself to them.

  5. 2022

    Finding my own faith

    Learning about numerology changed my life. It gave me clarity, and helped the people around me too. I chose a faith I truly believe in, not just the one I was born into. I went against what I was told, learned to discern what is true from what people believe is true, and now trust in a bigger force and the universe.

    "I have to believe what I was born into."

    My beliefs are mine to question, test and choose.

  6. 2025

    Penetration tester

    I wanted blue team because I thought it was easier, and kept saying I'd take any job except pentesting. No blue team job came, so I tried red team. I was terrified of OSCP because I feared failing, but I persevered and got it. Now I'm a pentester at Vantage Point Security, and I love consultancy: every client is different people and a new problem. I earn less than some of my uni peers, but I'm learning how to manage clients, vendors and stakeholders, and that will pay off for the rest of my career.

    "I'll fail, so I shouldn't even try."

    The job I said I'd never take turned out to be the one I love. Fear was pointing at exactly where I needed to grow.

  7. 2026

    Married, against all odds

    I married the love of my life, someone my faith and family said I shouldn't choose. We live away from my family now, and I'm happier than I've ever been. I'm glad I took the path less travelled.

    "I have to live the life other people expect of me."

    My life is mine to own. Love is worth choosing, even when it costs you.

Three lenses to understand yourself

Strengths & DISC

CliftonStrengths shows what comes naturally to you. DISC shows how you behave and communicate, and how others do. Together you stop fixing weaknesses, build on talent and work better with people.

Mine: Connectedness, Input, Communication, Strategic, Individualisation. DISC: Yellow and Green (Influence and Steadiness).

Numerology

Used as a mirror for reflection, not a prediction. Your numbers become questions: does this sound like you, and why?

I wrote an independent study on numerology and identity, and built a calculator.

Books

I've read a lot of self-help so you don't have to. You get the one book that fits where you are, and a plan to act on it.

The three that changed my life: The Code of the Extraordinary Mind (it taught me to spot "Brules", the bullshit rules, and stop living other people's expectations), Surrounded by Idiots and Positive Intelligence.

Questions

Who is this for?

Fresh grads and young professionals who feel conflicted between choosing themselves and what their family, community or friends expect. If you want to build a life you're proud of, on your own terms, this is for you.

Do I have to believe in numerology?

No. We use it as a prompt for reflection, not a prediction. If it doesn't resonate, we lean on your strengths and the books instead.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching is about moving forward. If you're struggling with your mental health, please reach out to a professional, and I'm happy to point you to support.

In person or online?

I prefer meeting face to face in Singapore, but online over video works just as well.
