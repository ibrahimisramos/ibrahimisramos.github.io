We know a flag starts with `IBR`. How far is it from `I` to `P`? - In CyberChef, use the **ROT13** recipe but change its **Amount** from 13. Try different numbers until `PIY` becomes `IBR`. learned: | The **Caesar cipher** shifts each letter by the same amount. With only 25 possible shifts, you can try them all, which is called a **brute-force** attack. Knowing part of the message (`IBR{`) made it even faster, a trick called a **known-plaintext** attack. real_world: | Weak encryption gets broken the same way: by trying every key, or by using parts of the message you can guess. That's why modern encryption uses keys with more combinations than there are atoms on Earth. ---
