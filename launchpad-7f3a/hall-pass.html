This page trusted the cookie `role=student` to decide what you could see, so changing it to `admin` gave you admin access. real_world: | Real websites use cookies to keep you logged in. If a site stores something like your role or user ID in a cookie *without protecting it*, attackers can change it. Testing this is a standard part of every web pentest. ---
