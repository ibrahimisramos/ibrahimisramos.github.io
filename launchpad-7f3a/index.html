This is a beginner capture-the-flag (CTF). Each challenge hides a flag that looks like CTF{something_here}. Find it, paste it in, and you'll unlock a short lesson on what you just did.

Everything works in your browser. The tools you'll need:

  • View source: right-click a page → "View page source" (or Ctrl+U)
  • Developer Tools: press F12 (or right-click → "Inspect")
  • CyberChef: a free website for decoding and analysing data
