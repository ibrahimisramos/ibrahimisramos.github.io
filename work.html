How an engagement with me goes

  1. 01Scope itAgree what's in bounds and what "bad" looks like for your business.
  2. 02Break itTest like an attacker, chain small bugs into real impact.
  3. 03Explain itA report and debrief that developers and leadership both understand.
  4. 04Fix itHelp with remediation, then retest to prove it's closed.

What I bring as a consultant

Broad offensive coverage

Web, API, mobile, thick-client and cloud testing, plus host configuration reviews, backed by OSCP+, BSCP, CRT and ACRTP.

A defender's eye

I started in the MHA and CSA SOCs, so my findings come with remediation that a blue team can actually act on.

Root causes, not just bugs

When one endpoint is broken, I look for the pattern behind it, so clients fix the class of issue and not just one instance.

Clear communication

Reports and debriefs that make sense to developers and leadership alike. Years of facilitating and training help here.

Privacy-aware

As a CIPT, I connect technical findings to data protection obligations, not just CVSS scores.

Builds what's missing

Custom tooling when off-the-shelf fails, like a Burp extension for end-to-end encrypted traffic and a JOSE fuzzing framework.
